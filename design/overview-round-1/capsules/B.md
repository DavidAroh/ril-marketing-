# Capsule B — Editorial broadsheet

- Premise: a front page, not an app — decisions as column inches, the day's tallies as a right-hand ledger of large serif numerals.
- Fonts: Georgia/"Times New Roman" serif throughout (400 only — hierarchy from size and italics); no mono. License: system. Fallback: Times.
- Scale: h1 88/0.98 with full-stop period; headlines 20/1.35 400; meta 13 italic; col-heads 11 uppercase +0.22em; big numerals 76/1.
- Case: sentence case; period after the title as typographic full stop; uppercase only on column heads.
- Palette: canvas #ffffff; ink #111111 primary; #55524a secondary prose; #8a8578 italic metadata; #14346e muted blue ink for headlines-as-links, big numerals, and emphasis; #a03323 deep red only on hot stamps; #c9c4b8 zero-states. Four roles + zero-state.
- Signature relationship: serif italic metadata line closing every entry ("Signal emerging · Review"); a right-hand rail where 76px serif numerals stack as a ledger.
- Invariants: serif-only voices; two-column broadsheet split (58/42); blue numerals; italic meta; the closing italic principle line.
- Assumptions/risks: 88px title assumes generous viewport; Georgia 400-only hierarchy depends on spacing discipline.
- Round 2 translation: keep column inches for decisions and ledger rail for counts; no cards or panels — spacing alone separates; links stay blue serif.
