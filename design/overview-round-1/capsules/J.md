# Capsule J — Wire service

- Premise: telemetry ticker — the page as dense rows on near-black, tabular rhythm like a wire service feed, lime on black for anything live.
- Fonts: system sans (600 for row titles); Consolas mono for every label, count, timestamp, and action. License: system.
- Scale: tick numerals 42 mono 700; row titles 15.5/1.4 600; micro 10–11 mono uppercase +0.16em; week strip 12 mono.
- Case: uppercase everywhere in machine voice (stamps, labels, actions, dates); sentence case row titles.
- Palette: canvas #101210; text #e6e9e2 primary; #7e857a secondary mono voice; #d8e84a lime on live counts, live stamps, and actions; #ff7a5c coral reserved for the failure. Three active roles + alert.
- Signature relationship: full-width four-column wire rows (number / title / stamp / action) on a strict baseline; a lime tick strip of counts under the masthead.
- Invariants: dark canvas; lime = live/attention; four-column row rhythm; mono-dominant voice.
- Assumptions/risks: highest density in the set — hardest to soften; lime-on-black is assertive and may overshadow brand blue entirely.
- Round 2 translation: rows become real interactive records with hover/focus states; the tick strip persists under the header; density reduces to 12 rows maximum per band.
