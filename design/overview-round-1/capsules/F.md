# Capsule F — Vertical week

- Premise: time is the spine — the seven days run down the page as the page's leftmost column, and everything else (decisions, workspace counts) hangs beside them.
- Fonts: system sans (400/600/700); Consolas mono for date, status stamps, and numerals. License: system.
- Scale: h1 40/700; corner numeral 58/700; day numerals 30/700 (zeros 400); workspace numerals 34 mono 400; titles 15/600; micro 10–11 uppercase +0.24em.
- Case: sentence case titles; uppercase labels/stamps.
- Palette: canvas #fbfaf7; ink #16211d primary (deep green-black); single secondary #0c6b4a (pine green) on status stamps, live day counts, and every action line; zeros stay ink. Two-color system.
- Signature relationship: the week as a vertical spine with live days picked out in green; a corner "3 decisions waiting" clock facing the title.
- Invariants: vertical day spine; two colors only; green = "needs you / is live"; corner counter; right column of mono tallies with action lines.
- Assumptions/risks: vertical spine consumes height — seven days plus queues needs scrolling on small screens; green-as-decision reads unconventional vs red/green semantics (failed post also green here — deliberate inversion, flag for human check).
- Round 2 translation: spine becomes the persistent week rail; decisions column and tallies column stay text-only; green carries all interaction color.
