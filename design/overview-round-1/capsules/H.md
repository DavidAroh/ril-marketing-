# Capsule H — Ghost tallies

- Premise: counts as presence — four enormous pale numerals sit behind/above their ink captions like stamps on a bench; the page's hierarchy is ghost vs ink.
- Fonts: system sans (400/600/700/800); Consolas mono for rail tallies and date. License: system.
- Scale: ghost numerals 120/800 −0.05em; ink captions overlap −34px into them; item titles 17/1.4 600; h1 22 uppercase +0.14em; micro 10–11 +0.24em.
- Case: uppercase structure; sentence case item text.
- Palette: canvas #faf7f2; ink #2b2320 primary; #7d7469 secondary metadata; #a08a76 tertiary labels/date; #c2570f terracotta accent on statuses, CTAs, and live week counts; #e9dfd2 pale clay ghost numerals (quaternary presence, ~8% of visual weight). Four active roles + ghost field.
- Signature relationship: 120px ghost numerals with ink captions overlapping their lower third — every metric is a two-layer typographic event.
- Invariants: ghost/ink overlap; terracotta as the only accent; no rules, boxes, or cards; uppercase colheads.
- Assumptions/risks: overlap risks legibility on some displays (mitigated: ghost is very pale); ghost numerals are decorative-adjacent — but they are the real counts, set in type.
- Round 2 translation: ghost tallies become the overview's header band; overlap constant (−34px) scales with the numeral; terracotta stays the only accent.
