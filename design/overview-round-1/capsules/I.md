# Capsule I — Marginalia

- Premise: a scholar's sheet — all metadata lives in a wide left margin (date, tallies, the week), while the main column carries only what needs judgement. Text as marginalia vs body.
- Fonts: system sans (400/600/700); Consolas mono for the margin date and content-column kicker. License: system.
- Scale: h1 42/700 −0.02em; item titles 18/1.42 600; margin numerals 30/700; margin labels 10 +0.18em uppercase; margin week list 12/2.1; micro 10–12.5.
- Case: sentence case content; uppercase margin labels and stamps.
- Palette: canvas #f5f4f8 (cool paper); ink #23222b primary; #6f6c7e secondary; #8b8798 tertiary margin voice; #6d5bd0 violet accent on the content kicker, status stamps, and action links; #b3413a red on publish-failed only. Four active roles.
- Signature relationship: a 240px left margin holding every number and the week, so the reading column contains nothing but decisions; violet reserved for "what you can act on".
- Invariants: margin/body split (240px/1fr); violet = actionable; margin carries all counts; no rules or panels.
- Assumptions/risks: wide-margin layout collapses poorly under 900px (Round 2 must define the mobile fold); violet is untested against the brand's blue.
- Round 2 translation: margin becomes the sticky left rail; on mobile the margin stacks above content in the same order; violet swaps to brand blue only if the human asks.
