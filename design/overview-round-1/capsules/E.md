# Capsule E — Split register

- Premise: two voices on one page — an ALL-CAPS operational register for machine states, and a calm human column that explains what wants your judgement in plain prose.
- Fonts: system sans throughout; 400/600/700; Consolas mono only for the human column's meta lines. License: system.
- Scale: strip numerals 40 mono 700; caps item titles 18/1.4 600; human intro 22/1.45; human rows 15/1.55; micro stamps 10 +0.26em; meta 12 +0.14em uppercase.
- Case: the left column is ALL-CAPS machine voice (statuses, meta); the right column speaks in sentences. The contrast is the design.
- Palette: canvas #f6f2e8 (newsprint cream); ink #221f1a primary; #6d675c secondary; #8c8271 tertiary caps; #1d4ed8 blue accents on every Review/REVIEW action; #a2321f red stamps. Four active roles.
- Signature relationship: same facts twice — staccato caps register left, explanatory prose right — so the page reads as both terminal and memo.
- Invariants: the two-register split; caps machine voice; blue actions; red stamps; full-bleed top with tall numeral strip.
- Assumptions/risks: duplication of content between registers could feel redundant if copy isn't genuinely differentiated; needs a wide viewport.
- Round 2 translation: keep registers as two components (state list + briefing column); caps voice for stamps/meta only; prose column written fresh per day.
